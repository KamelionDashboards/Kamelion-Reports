Next Steps · Derived From May 2026
Skills & Knowledge
39 starts in May - strongest since February. Content 74.7% in My Character and My Mind all year.
May's surge in starts is an opportunity. The year's content data shows students gravitating consistently towards My Character and My Mind. The question is whether May's starts reinforce this or shift towards less-engaged categories like Social Life (13.7%).
Before end of May, check with your Kamelion contact which content categories are driving the 39 starts. If Social Life is underrepresented, this is the month to prompt in form time.
Which content categories are May's 39 challenge starts concentrated in - and does that match your PSHE priorities for summer term?
Peer & Organisational Connection
All 39 May starts from Student Requests. NHS main school: mental gym only (12 sessions). Two groups engaging differently.
SR students are starting challenges at a high rate. NHS main school students are using mental gym but not starting challenges. This split is worth addressing before the summer break.
Use form time or PSHE this month to prompt NHS main school students - even one challenge started before summer keeps the habit alive for Year 2.
What would it take to ensure NHS main school students are as active on challenges in May as the Student Requests group?
Participation & Readiness
39 started · 16 completed so far · 84.1% year-to-date rate · month still live.
May's 39 starts are showing 41% completion so far because the month is live. End of term is approaching. Students with open challenges could close them before summer - a simple prompt could lift the final figure.
Identify which students started a challenge in May but have not yet completed. A single nudge before end of term could materially improve the May completion number going into Year 2.
How will you support students to close their May challenges before the end of term?
